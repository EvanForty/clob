From launch to buyback

Six steps from a new token to a running exchange with its own revenue.

launchToken()

Launch a coin and its order book

Create a token and a dedicated trading market is deployed with it automatically.

configureMarket()

Set your market's rules

Choose the trading pair, tick size, maker and taker fees, and minimum order size.

placeOrder()

Traders post limit orders

Bids and asks rest in the book at the prices traders choose, then match when they cross.

quote()

Add a market-making agent

Optional agents keep two-sided quotes in the book and manage their own inventory.

collectFees()

Every trade earns fees

A configurable share of each market's fee revenue goes toward its token.

dashboard()

Watch the market live

Track depth, volume, spreads, active traders and revenue on a public dashboard.

Pump.fun made token creation open to anyone. CLOB does the same for exchanges.

Instead of launching a token that depends entirely on someone else's venue, creators launch it with trading infrastructure they configure. More order books bring more traders, more volume means more maker and taker fees, those fees fund $CLOB buybacks, and the larger market draws in more creators and liquidity.

Buybacks come only from realized fee revenue. Nothing here promises a price outcome.
